THREE scopes, and they NEST. Three other categories are not scopes at all.

  PROMPT      typing. The default, and where you return to
  TRANSCRIPT  an entry is focused
  BLOCK       inside one — a table's rows, a widget's value, a camera

  ⇧⇥   OUT of the prompt, into the transcript — what shift-tab does
       in every form on the web
  ⇥    forward through the ring, and back to the prompt
  esc  RESOLVE THE INNERMOST TEMPORARY STATE, safely. Its order:
       1  a question is open      take its declared safe path
       2  a substate is open      close find, completion, history search
       3  the prompt has text     clear the line
       4  otherwise              out one rung: block → transcript → prompt
  an attached PTY is capture, not an ordinary substate: esc belongs to the
  child and the displayed host.detach action leaves it.

  “out one scope” was only the fourth case. The one-line version was
  true of the common path and wrong about the other three.

★ and the three categories that are NOT rungs on this ladder

  A BLOCKING OWNER is MODALa question may raise one; it takes input
                           until resolved. OVERLAY describes how a
                           layer paints, not what kind of owner it is
  A TAB is PARALLEL        a subagent — its own prompt, its own
                           context, its own three scopes. ⌃⇥ moves
                           SIDEWAYS, not up
  FIND and COMPLETION are  the prompt, relabelled. They expand it
  PROMPT SUBSTATES         and ⏎ targets something else

  an earlier pass listed five scopes including a pushed VIEW. Pushed
  views were then removed and the list was not. Worse, it was hiding a
  modelling error: NESTING, MODALITY and PARALLELISM are three
  relationships, and they were wearing one ladder.

scrolling is not a scope

  ⌥↑ ⌥↓, the wheel and the trackpad scroll WITHOUT moving focus.
  The prompt keeps it and you keep typing — the single biggest thing
  terminal apps get wrong.
