and THAT is the point: the logs are still on screen while you ask
about them. A pushed view would have taken them away at exactly the
moment you wanted to refer to them.

  the level column is a TONE, never a ground — a wall of painted
  labels would make the messages the quietest thing on screen.

  following means the tail pulls the block. Page up inside it once
  and it stops; the head says ● paused; returning to the bottom
  resumes it — which is Scroll.follow, not a new mechanism.
