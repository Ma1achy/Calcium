the spinner is chosen by what is happening, not at random

  ✦  thinking          the agent's walk  not a verb — see above
  ✦  reading a file    braille          the neutral default
  ✦  writing           growHorizontal   something is filling up
  ✦  searching         orbit            sweeping a space
  ✦  compiling         noise            churning
  ✦  network           bounce           a packet, out and back
  ✦  uploading         growVertical     rising
  ✦  waiting on you    toggle           two states, slow — nobody is busy
  ✦  waiting on a lock circleQuarters   blocked WORK, still retrying
  ✦  running tests     triangle         stepping through

and the ramp under it varies with the verb too — shimmer for work,
scatter for thinking, chase for a stream, breathe when it is your turn.

chosen deterministically from a small declared family per verb, so calls
may vary without changing meaning. Never random across the whole table.
