   owner       raised by            takes                  leaves by

★ EVERY ROUTED ACTION GETS ONE DECISION AT EACH RUNG.

  HANDLE             this is the one owner that acts
  REJECT             consume it and explain when silence would look broken
  PASS               continue downward
  GLOBAL-INTERCEPT   take the registry-declared exception

  a blocking question handles its answer actions and REJECTS unrelated
  typing; it never passes keys into the held prompt. Esc still resolves
  exactly one temporary owner.
  While it is open, pointer activation may commit only answer or inspection
  targets. Hits on the held scope are consumed without action. Wheel/page
  scroll needs a declared question/inspection viewport or background-scroll policy.

global-intercept candidates use an explicit OWNER-APPLICABILITY table

  interrupt   CHILD handles; QUESTION and COPY MODE reject; an ordinary
                running owner handles tool/turn interrupt; idle rejects silently
  page-scroll COPY MODE rejects while frozen; otherwise the active viewport
                handles without moving focus
  the wheel   is not delivered in COPY MODE or while mouse tracking is off;
                otherwise its pointer-hit owner handles it

  these are declared overrides, not contradictions in the ladder. Apps
  may register one only with an owner, fallback and collision test.

★ EVERY OWNER SAYS SO, in the footer’s last line

  ⏎ send   ⇧⏎ newline   ⇥ complete   ⇧⇥ transcript
   inside   ←→ orbit   ↑↓ tilt   o auto   r reset   esc out
   find   ↑↓ hits   ⏎ open   esc close   3 of 14
   question   declared actions   esc safe path
   copy   ←→↑↓ extend   ⏎ copy   esc out · the screen is frozen
   attached   keys → child   ⌃] host escape   ⌥esc enhanced detach

  AN OWNER YOU CANNOT SEE IS AN OWNER YOU WILL FIGHT. Every rung retains
  owner plus its highest-ranked reachable safe action. Ordinary rungs also
  show primary action, safe exit and help; the complete map is reachable.

★ A TAB IS ITS OWN FRAME — its transcript, prompt, scroll AND focus

  ⌃⇥ away and back finds it exactly as you left it. That persistence is
  what makes a tab a TAB rather than a view toggle — if switching lost
  your place, esc would want to leave it.
