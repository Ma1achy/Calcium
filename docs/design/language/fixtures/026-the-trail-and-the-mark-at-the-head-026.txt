★ two things a terminal CANNOT do, and both were tried

  a per-character FADE OVER TIME. There is no per-cell timeline — you
  repaint, so an age-based fade means every character in the trail
  repainted every frame. At a 100ms window that is ten repaints per
  character; a 500-character reply is 5,000 cell writes a second.

  a glyph RISING INTO its cell. There is no sub-cell position to
  animate toward. A character is at a cell or it is not.

  what IS affordable is a fixed band at the head: only the cells
  entering and leaving it are repainted, so the cost is a function of
  the BAND WIDTH and not of the reply length — about two repaints per
  cell at a one-cell band, four at three.
