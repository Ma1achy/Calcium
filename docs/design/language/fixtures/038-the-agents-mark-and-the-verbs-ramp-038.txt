two vocabularies, and the split is the whole design

  THE AGENT'S MARK   the bloom family, walked continuously
                     it means the agent is ALIVE and nothing more,
                     so varying it carries no claim

  A TOOL'S SPINNER   chosen by the verb
                     it means THIS PARTICULAR THING is happening,
                     so it stays inside the verb's declared family every time

  randomise what carries no information. Determine what does.

the agent's mark — four named subsets plus fullramp, one continuous walk

  ✦  updating the tests

  grow       ✦✢✲✶✷✹✺✹✷✶✲✢
  bloom      ⋅✧✦✢✻✾❀✿❀✾✻✢✦✧
  starfield  ✶✷✸✹✺✹✸✷
  pulse      ✢✲✱✻✱✲
             82 frames, 9.8s, and it does not repeat for ten seconds

  fullramp leads because it begins at a DOT — the walk opens small
  and blooms, rather than arriving mid-flower.

  it DRIFTS between families rather than picking one — which looks
  varied and is still a pure function of the tick.

  a RANDOM spinner cannot be golden-framed. Every frame in this
  repository is byte-compared, so a random choice would make the one
  thing on screen that moves the one thing no test can check.

  and the bloom family is RESERVED — fullramp, grow, bloom, starfield
  and pulse mean THE MODEL IS WORKING — its turn, or compacting its own

  history. A tool never blooms.
  which is still checkable: a bloom set on a TOOL is a defect a grep finds.

the ramp is chosen by the verb, because it describes the action

  ✦ searching the tree              wave      sweeping across
  ✦ writing parse.ts               chase     directional, laid down
  ✦  working out the depth rule    scatter   arriving out of order
  ✦ running the suite              tide      repeated passes across the suite
  ✦ compiling the crate            shimmer   steady work
  ✦ waiting on the registry        drift     out of our hands
  ✦ uploading the image            marquee   travelling, wraps
  ✦ waiting for your answer        breathe   waiting on YOU, not the machine
  ✦ retrying · attempt 2          pulse     something failed
  ●  committed a3f9b21               sweep     one pass, then it ends

  ten verbs, ten ramps, and each one's motion is an argument about
  the work: a search sweeps, a write advances, a thought arrives out
  of order, a wait on YOU breathes where a wait on the NETWORK drifts.

  so a reader who is not looking directly at the line still knows
  roughly what is happening — which is the whole point of motion in
  a surface that is mostly still.

and what does NOT get a semantic ramp

  a settled call        ● pytest · 4.2s · 47 passed
  state-bearing chrome  ▾ thinking · 4s · 312 tok
  the reader's own text ❯ refactor the parser
  a finished todo       ◼ read the current implementation
  below 8-bit colour  interpolation stops; discrete glyph motion remains
  motion reduced      ambient/nonessential ramps stop
  motion off          all animation stops; state stays in mark, label and elapsed
