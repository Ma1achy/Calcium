ONE HIERARCHY, with independent owners and target classes beside it

  owner route: frame / tab  →  scope  →  substate  →  focused element
  independent target: a selection range or set may overlap the focused element

  a FRAME     a tab. Its own prompt, context and scopes. ⌃⇥ sideways
  a SCOPE     prompt · transcript · block. Three, and they nest
  a SUBSTATE  find, completion, history search. It handles first, then passes
              to the prompt scope below it
  an ELEMENT  what has focus inside the scope
  a SELECTION what would be copied

  and a blocking QUESTION sits BESIDE the hierarchy and owns input while
  it is open. It is not a rung, which is why esc RESOLVES its declared
  safe path rather than leaking into the held scope.
  A captured CHILD also sits beside it: an attached PTY transfers ownership
  without becoming a substate; only host.detach returns ownership.

★ A QUESTION IS ONE IDENTITY WITH INDEPENDENT PROPERTIES

  answerForm · placement · blocking · owner · dismissal · safeResolution
  inspectionPath · editorState · residue

  CHOICE             normally REPLACES; ←→ and ⏎ choose an answer
  TEXT               normally FLOATS above the held prompt and uses
                     a question-scoped editor

  PANEL, PEEK and OVERLAY describe layer behaviour, not question type.

★ TWO TARGET CLASSES, which settles the click argument

  DIRECT-ACTION    a link, a button, a chip, a toggle, a legend entry,
                   a choice. ONE CLICK acts. There is nothing to be
                   inside, so focus-then-activate would be ceremony
  FOCUS-FIRST      a row, a plot, an expandable block, a slider. The
                   first click FOCUSES, the second ACTIVATES — because
                   entering one changes what the arrows drive

  the earlier text said both, in different sections, without naming
  the axis. The axis is whether the target HAS AN INSIDE.

★ FOCUS IS VISIBLY INTENSIFIED, by a SHAPE-SPECIFIC treatment

  an unpainted RUN or BOX inverts — its own tone becomes its ground
  a FRAME          its border or axes take the tone and go heavy
  a CONTROL        a wash over label, track and value
  a PAINTED IDENTITY chip bolds and takes a mark; the hue is its
                   identity and must not move

  the old “active means invert, everywhere” claim was true of two of the four, and
  the document drew the other two correctly while claiming otherwise.

★ HOVER MAY PREVIEW. It never moves focus, activates or commits domain state.

  a link, a button, a chip   previews its focus treatment
  a cursorable plot          moves the readout
  everything else            nothing

  “nothing, everywhere else” was already false in three sections. The
  rule that survives is the CONSEQUENCE, not the coverage.

★ IN THE ENTRY GUTTER, ● IS A WORK UNIT. The COLUMN says whose.

● I'll check the parser first.                  col 0 — the agent
● read_file(parse.ts) · 184 lines       col 0 — its call
  ├─ ●  count   verify the count       indented — a child's
    ● 7c2d4e1  decoder-zoom      succeeded      a table row

  “● is always the agent” was never true — it marks a unit of work,
  and the gutter column plus the indentation say who owns it.

★ PASSIVE against REJECTED, which is the honest form of the last rule

  PASSIVE — unconsumed, silent   an unarmed release, a right-click, a click
                                 on blank space, hover on nothing
  REJECTED — explains itself     a key with no action HERE, a
                                 disabled control, a refused paste

  “nothing silently does nothing” meant REJECTED. A pointer event with
  no target was never a command, and explaining it would be noise.
