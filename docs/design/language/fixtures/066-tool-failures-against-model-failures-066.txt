a tool failed and the MODEL failed are different facts, and the shapes differ

a TOOL failure is a result — it hangs under its own call and the turn goes on
  ● read_file(missing.ts) · 0.1s · failed
    ⎿ ✗ ENOENT: no such file
  ● I'll check the directory listing instead.     ← the turn continues

a MODEL failure ends the turn, and it is the status block
  ● The parser tracks quotes with a boolean, so a nes

  ──────────  ERROR  ──────────
  ✗ context exhausted at 50,000 tokens

  ✦ retrying in 8s · attempt 2        ← and it retries, visibly

four model failures, four answers

  rate limited      retry with backoff, and SAY the wait
  context exhausted offer compaction — it is actionable
  refused           not an error. The model answered
  transport died    the status block, and the partial reply STAYS

  a refusal is the model's answer and gets no error treatment:
  ● I won't do that — here is why, and what I can do instead.

  conflating the two teaches a reader to ignore red.
