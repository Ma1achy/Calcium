STRUCTURE
  Keep one durable event record, one rendered window, and one model context. Eviction changes the window; compaction changes context; neither erases history.  [R-COR-001]
  A call has one head and one gutter, with at most one bounded body when output exists.  [R-STR-001]
  At transcript level, one blank row separates sibling entries; spacing inside a body belongs to that block.  [R-STR-002]
  Every nested level costs three columns, and nesting is limited by width while the deepest child still clears its own minimum width.  [R-STR-003]
  Content stops one column before the inline-end edge.  [R-STR-004]

COLOUR
  Framework status red means failure and override red means unsafe override; red is never the only carrier. Syntax, removal, and identity retain their declared roles.  [R-COL-001]
  The tone budget is regional: an entry has three tones, while code, plots, and patches carry their own adjacent palettes.  [R-COL-002]
  A ground is for an extent; a tone is for a mark. Things take a ground and facts about things take a tone.  [R-COL-003]
  An actionable, status, or interaction ground is never the only carrier; dense data owes a readout.  [R-COL-004]
  Focus intensifies by shape: an unpainted run or box inverts, a frame takes its border or axes, a control washes, and a painted identity chip bolds and marks.  [R-COL-005]

MOTION
  A specimen animation may change only the glyph in a cell or that cell’s colour, opacity, or brightness. Transform, translate, scale, rotate, and layout motion are forbidden. Per-character stagger is retained. Glyph-swap frames resolve through a one-cell capability rung; ambiguous Unicode falls back to registered one-cell ASCII frames.  [R-MOT-005]
  Motion says that state is changing or something is live. Semantic animation says what is happening, never merely look here.  [R-MOT-006]
  A call’s shape does not depend on its duration.  [R-MOT-007]
  The bloom family belongs to the agent and nothing else uses it.  [R-MOT-008]

DEGRADATION
  Every actionable, status, and interaction distinction has two independent carriers and survives without colour. Dense data may shed colour-only detail only when a named readout preserves the meaning.  [R-COR-003]
  Every semantic glyph declares a collision-free ASCII fallback, width by capability, and reservedCells equal to its widest representation. The renderer pads the selected representation to that reservation.  [R-GLY-001]
