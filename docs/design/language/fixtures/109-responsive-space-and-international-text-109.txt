WIDTH AND HEIGHT ARE BOTH CAPABILITIES. LANGUAGE IS NOT A WIDTH ASSUMPTION.

  WIDTH  comfortable   80+ columns
         compact       40–79 columns; lower-priority chrome sheds
         narrow        20–39 columns; tables linearize; choices stack
         postage-stamp under 20 columns; one safe action at a time

  HEIGHT comfortable   16+ rows
         compact       8–15 rows; body keeps owner and safe action
         postage-stamp under 8 rows; one safe action at a time

width and height each propose a rung; the MORE CONSTRAINED rung wins. Prompt growth is
capped against the live viewport, never a fixed ten rows. No rung may clip a
permission choice, failure reason, current owner or focused identity.

text operations name their coordinate system and locale contract

  caret / delete      extended grapheme clusters
  word movement       locale-aware boundaries, including languages without spaces
  wrapping            locale-aware line breaking, not whitespace alone
  bidi                logical inline-start/end; isolate code, paths, URLs and ids
  IME                 composition is one edit; commands do not fire while composing
  search / sort       declared case folding, collation and normalization
  numbers / dates     locale grouping, decimal separator, plural and duration

emoji ZWJ sequences, variation selectors, flags, skin tones and combining marks
remain one grapheme for ordinary editing. Labels measure intrinsically; mandatory
choices wrap or stack rather than truncate. Direction of reading is never assumed.
