the alphabets remain; the semantics are three independent axes

  quantity     capacity · progress · count
  granularity  continuous · segmented
  liveness     still · active · stalled

BUDGET and OPERATION are named PRESETS, not the only possible types.
Alphabet follows granularity; motion follows liveness. A six-hour training
run is progress · continuous · active, without pretending it is a capacity.

  ███████████████░░░░░░░░░   capacity · continuous · still
  ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱   progress · segmented · active

a capacity is a STATE you glance at. It persists rather than completing,
and it is read in the corner of the eye:
  ctx   ███████████████████████░░░░░░░  78%  39k/50k
  disk  ██████████████████░░░░░░  74%

the OPERATION preset starts and ends, and has a spinner beside it. Segments
are visibly separate only when the work has countable units:
  ✦ stepping    ▮▮▮▮▯▯▯▯▯▯  4 of 10
  ✦ compacting  ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱  3 of 5 turns
  ✦ indexing    ▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱  412 of 1,847 files
  ✦ uploading   ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱  84 of 100 MB

quantity plus liveness say whether a bar can finish. The alphabet says
only whether its measurement is continuous or segmented.

and both degrade, differently

  continuous ██████████░░░░░░   →   ##########------   ascii
  segmented  ▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱      →   [#][#][#][.][.] ascii
             the ASCII rung preserves granularity. A spinner or text status
             independently carries active or stalled liveness.

  ▰ and ▱ are narrowOnly, so at ambiguousWidth: "wide" they take the
  ascii pair too — measured, not assumed.
