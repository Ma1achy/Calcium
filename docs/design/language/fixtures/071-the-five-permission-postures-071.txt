five postures, five symbols — and NOT ONE of them is painted

  ❯ manual           grey    every call comes back to you — the prompt's own chevron
  ? accept-edits    purple  edits go; it still asks about commands
  ❙❙ plan          blue    nothing is written
  » auto             yellow  nothing asks — fast forward
  !! skip             red     no permission check at all

why none of them is painted, and it is the whole answer to the error problem

  THE ERROR TAG IS THE ONLY PAINTED LABEL THAT MEANS A STATUS.
    ────────  ERROR  ────────
  so a posture drawn in text can never be mistaken for one, however red
  it is. The distinction is the CHANNEL, not the hue — which is stronger
  than any amount of picking different reds.

  and skip does not flash. It is not asking for your attention every
  second — it is telling you what is true, once.

  skip earns its loudness with a CLAUSE, not a colour — the thing it
  adds is the sentence at the end of the line.

the symbols, every one measured before it was picked

  ❯    U+276F   narrow, no emoji form    the prompt's own mark
  ?    U+003F   ascii                   it still asks
  ❙❙   U+2759   narrow, no emoji form    ⏸ U+23F8 IS an emoji — the ⏺ trap again
  »    U+00BB   narrow                   fast forward
  !!   U+0021   ascii                   the only ASCII symbol, for the only
                                        posture that is a warning to YOU

  ‼ U+203C is an emoji and ⚡ is wide AND an emoji. Two plain
  exclamation marks need no table at all.

  ascii    > manual   ? accept   || plan   >> auto   !! skip
