 A TABLE
  entry        a call or command returned rows
  carriers     a painted header · ▾ on the sorted column · the residue row
  actions      ↑↓ rows · ⇥ to a header, ⏎ sorts · ⌃⇧C copies VALUES
  esc          out to the transcript, remembering the row
  motion off   unchanged
  1-bit        the header is inverse; ▾ survives; the focused row inverts
  residue      the window, its count, AND its sort — the order is not the
               document’s, so the residue says so

 A PATCH
  entry        the model edited a file
  carriers     the path rule · a COLOURED stat · −/+ markers · the gutter
  actions      ⏎ enters · ⌃⇧C copies a UNIFIED DIFF, not the painted rows
  esc          out to the transcript
  motion off   unchanged
  1-bit        the markers alone. Grounds gone, gutter tone gone, text plain
  residue      the hunks and the stat. ↺ revert while the turn is recent

 A PLOT
  entry        a command or a tool produced a series
  carriers     the figure · its AXES · a legend · a readout when cursored
  actions      ⏎ enters · ←→ crosshair or camera · 1–9 toggle a series · hover
               moves the readout and NEVER the focus
  esc          out; the camera and the cursor keep their positions
  motion off   a live plot stops repainting on tick; it still patches on data
  1-bit        braille survives; the series are told apart by the legend’s
               marks, never by colour
  residue      the figure, the final camera, the cursor where you left it

 AN ATTACHED PTY — the CHILD captures ownership over a block
  entry        ⏎ on a terminal block, deliberately
  carriers     the border goes heavy · the head says !! attached · the footer
               names the child and the reserved host escape
  actions      keys go to the child, including ⌃C and esc, except host.detach
  esc          goes to the CHILD. ⌃] is the base-terminal host escape;
               ⌥esc also detaches when the protocol distinguishes it
  motion off   unchanged
  1-bit        the heavy border is the carrier; the !! survives
  residue      the block, scrollable, no longer captured

 A SUBAGENT TAB
  entry        the model fans out
  carriers     a painted name chip · its hue on all four rules · the footer row
  actions      ⌃⇥ switches · ⌘1–9 jumps · its prompt is LIVE
  esc          its own three scopes. esc never leaves the tab — ⌃⇥ does
  motion off   its walk stops; its elapsed count carries liveness
  1-bit        the NAME carries identity; the hue is gone
  residue      its transcript stays. The parent records what came back

 A PASTE CHIP
  entry        a paste longer than a few lines, or an @ mention
  carriers     the chip · its size BEFORE you attach · a warn past a threshold
  actions      ⏎ opens it to edit · ⌥⌫ drops it · it travels with the message
  esc          closes the edit view, keeping the chip
  motion off   unchanged
  1-bit        the brackets carry it: [#1 json · 47L]
  residue      part of the sent message, and the transcript shows the chip

★ three more blank cells, and each was a real hole

  A TABLE’S RESIDUE had to say its SORT. The rows are not in the
  document’s order once you sort them, and a reader who scrolled past
  the header has no way to know.

  A PLOT’S 1-BIT form could not use colour to separate series, and
  nothing said what did. The LEGEND’S MARKS do — which means a plot
  with more series than marks is a plot that cannot degrade, and that
  is a real limit the plot arm should enforce.

  AN ATTACHED PTY’S ESC belongs to the CHILD. It alone sends esc outside
  Calcium; other captured owners handle it on the host, while passive rows
  may declare no-op or not-applicable. The PTY therefore needs its own
  chord and the footer has to say it. Without the table, that cell was
  simply never asked.
