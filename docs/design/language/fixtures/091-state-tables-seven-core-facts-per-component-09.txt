seven required facts per component, all checkable; axis-specific rows may extend them.

  ENTRY        what puts it on screen
  CARRIERS     what says what it is — and there are always at least two
  ACTIONS      what a key or a click does to it
  ESC          where esc lands, by the published order
  MOTION OFF   its form with no animation at all
  1-BIT        its form with no colour at all
  RESIDUE      what is left once it settles

  prose said all of this. A table makes a missing cell VISIBLE, which is
  the failure this document keeps finding — a rule stated in one section
  and quietly unstated in the next.

 A CALL
  entry        the model invokes a tool
  carriers     ● mark · verb · duration slot · result-summary slot · ⎿ gutter
  running      ● white STILL, spinner in the duration slot
  actions      ⏎ expand / enter · ⌃⇧C copies invocation AND output
  esc          out to the transcript
  motion off   the spinner is omitted; static ● plus elapsed carry liveness
  1-bit        ● still; outcome in bold; the gutter unchanged
  residue      ● ok · verb · duration · a NUMBER. Never “succeeded”

 A QUESTION — CHOICE
  entry        a tool, a command or the app needs an answer
  placement    declared; replace is the default · blocking yes · owner question
  carriers     ⟩ mark · focused choice painted · chosen answer keeps ✓ · safe path named
  actions      ←→ move focus · ⏎ answer · click a choice · inspect suspends
               the same unresolved question
  esc          takes the declared safe path: answer, cancel, or step back one arm
  motion off   unchanged — it never animated
  1-bit        › on the focused choice; ✓ remains on a chosen answer
  residue      question · terminal resolution · answer when one exists

 A QUESTION — TEXT
  entry        an answer needs a sentence, not a choice
  placement    float · blocking yes · owner question editor
  carriers     ⟩ and the question above the held prompt · shared editor code
  actions      ⏎ submits · ⇧⏎ newline · editing operations are shared;
               buffer, history and undo are question-scoped
  esc          cancels the QUESTION, not the text
  motion off   the caret stops blinking; nothing else changes
  1-bit        unchanged
  residue      question · terminal resolution · sentence when submitted

 A BOUNDED BLOCK
  entry        a call or a command produced more rows than fit
  carriers     the window · a RESIDUE ROW saying how many are hidden
  actions      ⏎ enters · ↑↓ rows · ⇧↑↓ selects · ⌃⇧C copies SOURCE
  esc          out to the transcript, remembering the row
  motion off   unchanged
  1-bit        the focused row inverts; the residue row is unchanged
  residue      the window and its count. It does not re-expand on its own

 A STREAMING REPLY
  entry        the model begins producing text
  carriers     the TRAIL at the head · the agent’s mark one space past it
  actions      ⌃C stops the turn and KEEPS the partial text
  esc          nothing — it is not a temporary state
  motion off   no trail, no bloom. The elapsed counter carries liveness
  1-bit        the trail is a BOLD band; the head band is SGR 7
  residue      settled text, no cursor. Nothing replaces the bloom

 FOCUS
  entry        ⇧⇥ from the prompt, ↑↓, a click, or restored from memory
  carriers     the shape’s own treatment · the ▸ mark · the footer’s key row
  actions      ↑↓ move · ←→ within a row · ⏎ enter or activate
  esc          out one rung
  motion off   unchanged — focus never animated
  1-bit        INVERSE, and the ▸ carries the rest
  residue      REMEMBERED by id. Inactive focus is the wash, not the paint

 A WATCH
  entry        a long-running job you started, or /watch
  carriers     the footer row · a bar · the entry it points AT
  actions      ←→ along the row · ⏎ scrolls to its entry and opens it
  esc          nothing — the footer is not a scope
  motion off   the bar stops shimmering; the percentage stays
  1-bit        the bar is ## against ..; the name carries identity
  residue      it DROPS ITSELF, and the completion earns a notification

 A NOTIFICATION
  entry        a blocked question · a turn over ~30s · a watched run ending
  carriers     the bell · OSC 9 · the title — three rungs, all opt-in
  actions      none. It is not on screen
  esc          not applicable
  motion off   unchanged
  1-bit        unchanged
  residue      the new-messages button, if you were scrolled away

★ two cells that were BLANK until this table existed

  a streaming reply’s MOTION OFF form. The trail and the bloom are
  both motion, and with both gone nothing said the reply was still
  arriving. The elapsed counter had to be promoted to carry it. Every live
  thing retains a non-motion liveness carrier; indeterminate or streaming
  work uses elapsed time.

  a watch’s RESIDUE. It drops itself, so being away meant it vanished
  with no trace. That is what put watched-run completion on the
  notification ladder.
