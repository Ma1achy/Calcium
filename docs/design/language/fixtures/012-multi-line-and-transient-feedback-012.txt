this framework has no FLOATING, RECORDLESS toast. A transient notice that
appears and vanishes away from its action is unreachable by keyboard and
unreadable by anyone who looked away. Feedback stays where the action was,
and any consequential fact is also written to the record.

AS AN ENTRY, for anything undoable

  because an undo you cannot see is an undo you will do twice.

the rule: a confirmation's LIFETIME matches its consequence

  nothing changed        the footer, briefly
  a selection changed    on the thing, until focus moves
  the document changed   an entry, forever — it is history now
