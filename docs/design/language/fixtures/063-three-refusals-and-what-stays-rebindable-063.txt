three things refused, and each is refused for the same reason

1 · NO LEADER KEYS, NO TWO-KEY SEQUENCES [R-REF-001]
  no ⌃x ⌃s, no g g, no space-then-f. A sequence is a MODE you are
  in for 400ms without being told — and the way you discover you
  were in it is that the next key did something else.

  every binding is one chord. The footer shows the highest-ranked live
  actions that fit; help shows all. A sequence cannot be shown; it can only be
  remembered.

2 · NO CHORDS THE PLATFORM ALREADY OWNS [R-REF-002]
  ⌥←→ is word movement. ⌘←→ is line ends. ⌘C is the terminal's
  copy. ⌃↑ is Mission Control. Taking one wins an argument with the
  reader's own machine, and the machine wins.

3 · NO KEY THAT ONLY WORKS SOMETIMES [R-REF-003]
  if ⏎ means open in the transcript, it opens in EVERY scope that
  has something to open, and it is absent from the footer where it
  does not. A key that silently does nothing in one place is worse
  than a key that does not exist.

and what is deliberately left rebindable

  every chord is rebindable. Confirm, safe exit, focus movement and
  interrupt are structural ACTIONS, so each must keep one reachable
  binding or named-command path after conflict resolution.

  ❯ /keys posture ⌥p
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 ⟩ ⌥p is already cycle posture. Replace it?
  › no    replace it    pick another chord
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
←→ move     ⏎ answer   — a conflict is a QUESTION like any other
  › no    replace it    pick another chord
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
←→ move     ⏎ answer   — a conflict is a QUESTION like any other

  a conflict is answered before it is written, not discovered when
  the old binding stops working.

prompt undo is the editor's, not the agent's

  ⌘Z        undo in the line editor — your typing only
  ⌘⇧Z       redo
  ↺ on an entry  revert what the AGENT did — a different thing,
                in a different place, with a different key

  sharing one undo between the two would mean ⌘Z sometimes touches
  your files, which is the kind of surprise nobody recovers trust
  from.
