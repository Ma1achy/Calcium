THREE TIERS, not two — and each degrades its own way

  CONTENT       shrinks to its FLOOR; representation substitution happens before
                any final container clip or scroll
                a plot, a table, a mosaic cell, a chip
                visible and recoverable

  REPRESENTATIONsubstitutes a smaller FORM saying the same thing
                a sidebar → a rail, a footer → its primary action

  DECORATION    truncates with NO MARK, then drops with no trace
                a rule, a spacer, a trailing dash, a box corner

★ TRUNCATION MARKS LOSS. A shorter rule has lost nothing.

  which is why decoration gets no ellipsis — and why its minimum is a
  THRESHOLD rather than a floor:

  content's min      a FLOOR — it WIDENS its container to keep it
  decoration's min   a THRESHOLD — it NEVER widens anything, and
                     below it the decoration simply goes

   7/10 ────────────────────────────── 39k/50k █████████░░░   56
   7/10 ────────── 39k/50k █████████░░░   40
   7/10       39k/50k █████████░░░      30
   7/10    39                                  22

  the rule fills, then silently goes — and the CONTENT keeps every cell
  it had. A cell that vanishes silently is a bug; a divider that vanishes
  silently is the design.

★ SHED, DON’T SHRED — and four kinds get this wrong today (C09 I81)

  truncation is for ONE RUN too long for its box. Shedding is for a ROW
  OF PEERS too many for the width. Four kinds apply truncation to a
  shedding problem, which is why every column gives at the same moment.

  today   basel… a      b      Δ     ✓   every part, together
  should  a b Δ ✓ +1               one at a time, in a declared order
          a b ✓ +2
          a b +3                     RANK 0 NEVER SHEDS

  comparison  the two VALUES never shed — without them it is not a
              comparison
  keyValue    the KEY never sheds — a value with no key is not a fact
  events      the TIME and the mark never shed
  steps       the CURRENT step never sheds

  and the last is the agent switcher’s own rule arriving at a fourth
  kind: a row that cannot show you where you are is worse than no row.
