  orbit, the box family and the rest are separate alphabets with their
  own frame counts, and they keep their own.

  ✦  braille   80ms      a different alphabet entirely

and NOTHING varies its rate at runtime

  no rung, no heuristic, no mapping from throughput to speed. A rate
